This Freelance Web Development & Partnership Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between the Freelance Web Developer ("Developer") and the purchasing entity or individual ("Client") initiating services via the Developer's web portal. By selecting a service package, making an initial deployment payment, or clicking "Get Started," the Client explicitly agrees to be legally bound by all terms, conditions, and provisions outlined below.

01 // Definitions & Coverage

"Project" refers to the custom engineering, styling, deployment, and configuration of the web infrastructure selected by the Client.

"Static Layout" refers to light, modern frontend code optimized for delivery over Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), operating with zero ongoing server overhead.

"Dynamic Architecture" refers to complex, custom-engineered web frameworks utilizing third-party application programming interfaces (APIs), databases, continuous cloud logic, or transactional operations.

"Scope Creep" defines any request, feature expansion, layout alteration, asset addition, or operational loop requested by the Client that falls outside the explicit parameters of the selected service block.

02 // Project Parameters & Service Blocks

The Client agrees to bind the Project to one of the following explicit operational frameworks:

A. Package 01: Social & Creator Tier
Scope: Deployment of a specialized 1-page custom structural layout engineered as an optimized, permanent link-in-bio hub.
Financial Structure: Flat, one-time payment of $199.00 USD due prior to project initialization.
Maintenance Boundary: Deployed onto permanent free static cloud hosting, yielding $0.00/month in mandatory hosting infrastructure fees.
Modifications: All post-handoff maintenance operates strictly under a "Pay-As-You-Go" framework billed at a fixed rate of $10.00 USD per discrete text or hyperlink change.

B. Package 02: Local Business Tier
Scope: Complete custom frontend development consisting of a 3-to-5 page high-speed, mobile-responsive grid layout, equipped with an advanced local Search Engine Optimization (SEO) baseline configuration.
Financial Option A (Payment Plan): Four (4) consecutive monthly installments of $149.00 USD. Upon completion of the 4-month payment term, ongoing infrastructure, monitoring, and content updates scale down to the standard $49.00 USD/month maintenance contract.
Financial Option B (Outright Purchase): Upfront setup payment of $649.00 USD, supplemented by a recurring $49.00 USD/month maintenance contract.
Operational Inclusions: Entitles the Client to a dedicated "Text-Me-Updates" helpline, covering up to one (1) collective hour of monthly content adjustments. Unused operational hours do not roll over to subsequent billing periods.

C. Package 03: Custom Architecture Tier
Scope: Bespoke technical engineering comprising structural parameters including, but not limited to, full-scale e-commerce modules, secure payment gateways, dynamic data routing, custom content management systems (CMS), and complex cloud database configurations.
Financial Structure: Governed by an independent, itemized project scope document and structured quote tailored to architectural complexity.
